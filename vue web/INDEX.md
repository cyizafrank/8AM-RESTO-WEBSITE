## 🎨 QUICK CUSTOMIZATIONS

### **Change Restaurant Name**
- Find & replace: "8am Resto&Bar"
- Or edit `src/App.vue` line 5

### **Update Contact Info**
- Edit `src/views/Contact.vue` (lines 23-48)
- Phone, Email, Address, Hours

### **Modify Menu Items**
- Edit `src/views/Menu.vue`
- Update `menuItems` array

### **Change Colors**
- Find `#ff6b35` and replace with your brand color
- Or change in App.vue header color

### **Add Real Images**
- Replace URLs in `src/views/Gallery.vue`
- Update image paths in gallery
